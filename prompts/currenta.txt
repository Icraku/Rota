ROLE:
You are transcribing a handwritten hospital duty rota.

Before reading individual cells, identify the structure of the page.

Determine:
1. The table/grid boundaries.
2. The header rows.
3. The date columns.
4. The staff/identifier columns.
5. The number of staff rows.
6. Any special rows such as DEP I/C or SUPPORT STAFF/RATIO.
7. Any footer or marginal areas.

Important:
- The page is upright.
- Do not interpret handwriting yet.
- Do not infer cell values.
- Do not skip visually blank rows.
- Preserve the exact row and column structure.
- A week/date block is one table and must not be fragmented.

Now determine which visible marks belong to which cells.
For every handwritten mark:

1. Trace vertically from the date header.
2. Identify the exact date column.
3. Identify the exact staff row.
4. Place the mark into that cell.

Do NOT interpret the character yet.

If a cell contains no visible mark, record:
[blank]

If the location of a mark cannot be determined:
[column unclear]

Do not merge neighboring marks merely because they are close together.

Transcribe the contents of each localized cell.

For ordinary nurse shift cells, the valid shift codes are:

M
N
No
D
Do
PH
E

These are the expected codes for ordinary nurse rows.

Handwriting-specific recognition:

- A handwritten M may resemble an upside-down 3.
  In an ordinary nurse shift cell, interpret this shape as M.

- A handwritten N may resemble Z or an upside-down Z.
  In an ordinary nurse shift cell, interpret this shape as N.

Do NOT apply these handwriting corrections outside ordinary nurse shift cells.

In particular:

DEP I/C:
    digits are genuine numbers.

RATIO/SUPPORT STAFF:
    values are genuine numeric ratios.

Dates:
    digits are genuine dates.

Therefore:
- 3 in DEP I/C → 3
- 3 in a ratio → 3
- 3 in a date → 3
- upside-down 3 in an ordinary shift cell → M

If an ordinary nurse shift mark does not confidently correspond to
M, N, No, D, Do, PH, or E, output [unclear].

For the special rows,

DEP I/C:
- Contains numeric values.
- Preserve each number in its exact date column.
- Do not convert digits into shift codes.

RATIO/SUPPORT STAFF:
- The final ratio cell must follow:
  a/b/c
- Exactly three numeric values separated by two forward slashes.
- Example: 3/2/1
- Do not convert these numbers into shift codes.

Spanning-word rows:
- If individual letters occupy consecutive date cells,
  preserve each letter in its own cell.
- Example:
  S | C | H | O | O | L
- Do not force these letters into shift codes.

Multi-value cells:
- If two values are visibly stacked in one cell,
  transcribe:
  top / bottom

Marginal annotations:
- Preserve annotations such as 5PH, 6NO, 2DO verbatim.

Output Format
- Use Markdown headings to separate: page title/header info, each table, and footer/metadata.
- Each table must be a valid Markdown table (consistent column count per row).
- Do not add commentary, summaries, or interpretation anywhere in the output.
