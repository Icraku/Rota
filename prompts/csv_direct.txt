For ordinary nurse shift cells, the valid shift codes are:

M meaning afternoon shift
N meaning night shift
No meaning night off
D meaning day shift
Do meaning day off
PH meaning public holiday
E evening shift

These are the expected codes for ordinary nurse rows.

Handwriting-specific recognition:

- A handwritten M may resemble an upside-down 3.
  In an ordinary nurse shift cell, interpret this shape as M.
- A handwritten N may resemble Z or an upside-down Z.
  In an ordinary nurse shift cell, interpret this shape as N.

Do NOT apply these handwriting corrections outside ordinary nurse shift cells.

In particular:

Dates: digits are genuine dates.

If an ordinary nurse shift mark does not confidently correspond to M, N, No, D, Do, PH, or E, output [unclear].

For the special rows,

DEP I/C:
- Preserve each value in its exact date column.
- Do not convert digits into shift codes or vice versa.

RATIO:
- The final ratio cell must follow: a/b/c
- Exactly three numeric values separated by two forward slashes.
- The values are genuine numeric ratios of the sum of evening shift (E) / sum of afternoon shift (M) / sum of night shift (N).
- Example: 3/2/1 in a ratio cell -> 3 counts of E/2 counts of M/1 count of N in that column.
- Do not convert these numbers into shift codes.

Spanning-word rows:
- If individual letters occupy consecutive date cells, preserve each letter in its own cell.
- Example: S | C | H | O | O | L
- Do not force these letters into shift codes.

Multi-value cells:
- If two values are visibly stacked in one cell, transcribe: top / bottom

Marginal annotations:
- Preserve annotations such as 5PH, 6NO, 2DO verbatim.
